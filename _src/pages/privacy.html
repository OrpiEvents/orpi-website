Who we are

ORPI Events Ltd (“ORPI”, “we”, “us”) runs mobile bars for weddings and events across London and England. We’re responsible for the personal information you give us. Questions about it go to {{EMAIL}}.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through this website, we collect:

  • your name, email address and phone number
  • your event details: the type of event, the date (or roughly when), guest numbers, the venue or town, and the services you’re interested in
  • how you heard about us, and who recommended us if you tell us
  • anything else you write in the form

We also note which of our links, posts or ads brought you to the form (for example “Instagram bio link”) and the page you arrived on. That tells us which of our marketing works; it isn’t used to identify you.

To stop spam and accidental double enquiries, we keep a scrambled (“hashed”) form of your email address and of your internet connection’s IP address. It can’t be turned back into the original.

If you book with us, we also keep your quote, invoices and payment records, your acceptance of our terms, and the choices you make in your ORPI app, such as drinks, timings and venue details. You sign in to the app with Apple, Google or a link sent to your email. We receive your email address (and your name, if you choose to share it), never your password.

How we use it

  • To reply and prepare your quote, and to plan and run your event if you book. We need your details to do what you’ve asked us to, and to carry out our agreement with you.
  • To follow up about your event, for example to check you received your quote, or to get back in touch as your date gets closer. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To send occasional planning tips and offers about our own services, unless you tick the box on the form to say no. You can opt out at any time by replying “stop” or emailing us.
  • To understand which of our marketing works, using the link and page information described above.

We never sell your details, and we don’t share them with anyone for their own marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

When you open and start the enquiry form, we count it, along with the link that brought you. That’s a count only: no cookies, and nothing that identifies you.

Who helps us

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the business:

  • Notion, where we keep enquiries and bookings
  • Supabase, our app’s database and sign-in, hosted in London
  • Vercel, which hosts this website and our app
  • Resend, which sends our emails

If you message us on WhatsApp or Instagram, those messages are handled by Meta under its own privacy policy, and we keep what we need to reply and prepare your quote.

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by the protections UK law requires, such as the UK–US data bridge or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

If you don’t book, we keep your enquiry until your event date has passed and any follow-up is finished, and no longer than two years after you enquired. If you book, we keep the booking and payment records for six years, because UK tax rules require it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to us using it for marketing. Just email {{EMAIL}}.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
