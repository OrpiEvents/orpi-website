What’s included in your service?

Everything you need for a fully running bar: our team, the equipment and bar tools, glassware, ice, mixers and garnishes (or we work with what your venue provides). We set up before your guests arrive and clear everything down at the end of the night. Your quote lists exactly what’s included, so there are no surprises.

Do you supply the alcohol, or do we?

Either works. On Premium and Ultimate we source and supply all the alcohol. On Bar Only, you supply the alcohol and we bring everything else, and we’ll advise you on quantities. If you only need us for arrivals, there’s Welcome Drinks.

How far in advance should we book?

Ideally at least a month before, so we can give your event the attention it deserves. If your date is sooner, get in touch anyway: we’ll do everything we can to make it work. Summer weekends go quickly, so earlier is always better.

How does pricing work?

Every event is different, so we don’t have a fixed price list. We build your quote around your guest numbers, package, drinks and venue, usually within 24 hours of your enquiry. A 50% deposit secures your date, and the balance is due 14 days before your event.

What if our guest numbers change?

Your quote is based on the guest numbers you give us when you book, and that becomes your minimum charge, so if final numbers come in lower the price stays the same. If numbers go up, we’ll agree the extra with you in writing. Please confirm final numbers at least 7 days before your event.

Can you look after guests who don’t drink?

Absolutely, and we do it properly. Our mocktails are made with the same care as our cocktails, and we pour Pentire, the alcohol-free spirit The New York Times has rated number one three years running, plus Quarter® Proof mid-strength spirits at 15% ABV.

Can we have our own cocktails?

Yes, and we love building them. Our menu is a starting point, not a limit. Bring us a favourite, a theme or a completely new idea and we’ll make it happen.

How many bar staff will you bring?

It depends on your guest numbers, the timings and the service style. We staff every event so nobody waits long at the bar, and we confirm the team size when we put your package together.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based in Ilford, East London, and cover events across England. Travel is built into your quote, so there are no surprise costs.

Are you insured and licensed?

Yes. We carry £5m public liability insurance and our team holds personal licences. Most venues have a premises licence that covers the bar. If yours doesn’t, you may need a Temporary Event Notice, so tell us when you book and we’ll talk you through it.

If your venue needs our insurance, licences or risk assessment, you can request them in your ORPI app.

What if something goes wrong on the day?

We plan thoroughly, arrive early and always have a backup. One of our favourite reviews is from a couple whose original bar company put its price up two weeks before their wedding: we stepped in with 13 days to go, ran a tasting at their home within days, and served their signature cocktails all night.
