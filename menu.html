Pentire

5 serves

Alcohol-free spirits from Cornwall, distilled from botanicals native to the Cornish coast and rated the number one non-alcoholic spirit by The New York Times three years running. Made to be enjoyed in its own right, not as a straight swap for alcohol.

  • Coastal Spritz

    Light, bitter and wonderfully aromatic. A naturally complex aperitif with coastal botanical depth, lifted by a gentle effervescence and a fragrant herbal garnish. The perfect alcohol-free opener.

  • Spicy Margarita

    Bold, citrusy and with a satisfying chilli kick. Bright lime and agave bring the classic margarita energy, underpinned by the earthy botanical depth of Pentire Adrift. Zero alcohol, full character.

  • Beach House Paloma

    Fresh and sun-drenched with a beautifully zesty edge. Pink grapefruit and lime shine alongside the coastal botanicals of Pentire Seaward for a clean, vibrant serve that needs nothing more than ice and good company.

  • Pentire Gimlet

    Crisp, sharp and quietly sophisticated. Fresh lime and a whisper of maple balance beautifully against aromatic coastal botanicals, finished with the subtle herbal note of fresh bay. Elegantly simple.

  • Pentire Negroni

    A reimagined classic with real depth and bitterness. Pentire Adrift and Pentire Coastal Spritz with non-alcoholic vermouth deliver all the richness and complexity of a traditional Negroni. Alcohol-free never tasted this good.

Quarter® Proof

5 serves

Mid-strength spirits at 15% ABV: the sweet spot between full strength (37.5% and up) and alcohol-free. Full-strength flavour for guests who’d rather pace the night.

  • Middy Margarita

    All the bold, citrusy character of a classic margarita at a fraction of the strength. Sharp lime and agave sweetness pair with a genuinely tequila-like base for a refreshing, easy-drinking twist on a timeless favourite.

  • Dirty Martini

    Savoury, sophisticated and stirred to perfection. Briny olive and dry vermouth bring real depth and complexity to a low-ABV grain spirit base. All the theatre of a proper martini, with a gentler finish.

  • Paloma

    Bright, citrusy and completely moreish. Fresh pink grapefruit and sharp lime shine alongside a light agave spirit for a clean, fizzy long drink that’s as refreshing as it is low in alcohol.

  • Espresso Martini

    Rich, smooth and indulgent with just enough of a kick to keep things interesting. Fresh espresso and coffee liqueur are balanced by a clean low-ABV vodka base for a cocktail that’s equal parts energising and elegant.

  • Blood Orange Spritz

    Vibrant, fruity and strikingly coloured. Bitter Italian aperitif notes and sweet blood orange come together with a light gin-style base and a gentle fizz. A beautiful aperitivo alternative that won’t slow the evening down.

Prepear

Before the night

Made from whole Shingo pears grown in Naju, South Korea, and rooted in Korean wellness traditions. Natural and vegan, with no preservatives, added sugar or artificial flavours: one ingredient, at its purest. For your guests to enjoy before the celebration.

  • Prepear

    A 100% natural pear drink, served to your guests before the night gets going. Ask us about adding it to your event.
